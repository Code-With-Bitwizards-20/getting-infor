System Architecture

How Digital Reconnaissance & Forensics Works

Sherlock probes public platforms while ExifTool extracts hardware, camera, timestamp, and GPS geolocation metadata from discovered media without requiring user credentials.

STEP 01

Identity Querying

Target usernames are parsed and probed concurrently across hundreds of verified platforms in Sherlock's database.

STEP 02

ExifTool Image Extraction

When a profile is confirmed, its avatar and top 5 photos are scraped and analyzed via ExifTool for camera tags, timestamps, and GPS geotags.

STEP 03

Deep OSINT & Identity Correlation

GitHub commit emails, public SSH keys, Gravatar linked accounts, and avatar MD5 hash matching tie profiles together.

STEP 04

Executive PDF Dossier

A multi-page, executive intelligence report with clickable profile and Google Maps links is generated on demand for download.
